The Fleet

Built to Protect

Gretna Fire & Rescue maintains a diverse fleet of front-line and reserve apparatus to meet the full spectrum of emergency response needs across our district. Our equipment is regularly inspected, maintained, and upgraded to ensure every unit is mission-ready at a moment's notice.

Since the 2019 merger of Gretna Volunteer Fire Department and Gretna Rescue Squad, the combined fleet has grown with the addition of two new engines and a new ambulance — ensuring we continue to meet the demands of our growing community.

All apparatus is housed at Station 22 at 122 E Gretna Rd, Gretna, VA 24557.

Fire Fleet

🚒 Engine 22 Fire Front Line
E-22

Engine 22

Primary Attack Pumper — first-due structural and vehicle fire response across the district.

Type Class A Pumper
Pump 1,500 GPM
Tank 500 Gallons
  • Structural & vehicle fire attack
  • Mutual aid response throughout Pittsylvania County
  • Wildland/interface fire capability
🚒 Engine 221 Fire Front Line
E-221

Engine 221

Second-due attack pumper supporting fire operations and rural water supply.

Type Class A Pumper
Pump 1,250 GPM
Tank 1,000 Gallons
  • Secondary attack & water supply
  • Large-tank rural area operations
  • Backup front-line pumper
🚒 Tanker 22 Fire Front Line
T-22

Tanker 22

Water supply tender for rural and non-hydrant operations across the district.

Type Water Tender
Pump 750 GPM
Tank 2,500 Gallons
  • Rural water supply & shuttle operations
  • Tender operations with dump tank
  • Mutual aid water supply support
EMS Apparatus

Medic Units

🚑 Medic 22 EMS Front Line
M-22

Medic 22

Primary ALS ambulance staffed 24/7 by certified EMTs and paramedics for emergency medical response and patient transport.

Type Type I ALS Ambulance
Level Advanced Life Support
Crew EMT-B / Paramedic
  • Advanced cardiac monitoring & 12-lead ECG
  • IV/IO therapy, medication administration
  • Emergency medical transport to Sovah Martinsville
  • Cardiac arrest resuscitation & airway management
🚑 Medic 221 EMS Reserve
M-221

Medic 221

Reserve ALS ambulance available when M-22 is committed, ensuring continuous EMS coverage for the district.

Type Type I ALS Ambulance
Level Advanced Life Support
Crew EMT-B / Paramedic
  • Full ALS capability matching primary unit
  • MCI & multi-unit EMS response support
  • EMS standby events & community coverage
  • Activated when M-22 is out of service or committed

Support Fleet

🛻 Rescue 22 Support Front Line
R-22

Rescue 22

Heavy utility and rescue vehicle carrying specialized equipment for extrication, technical rescue, and scene support operations.

Type Heavy Utility / Rescue
Role Technical Rescue
  • Vehicle extrication with hydraulic rescue tools

🌲 Brush 22 Support Front Line
BR-22

Brush 22

4x4 off-road capable unit designed for grass, brush, and wildland-interface fire suppression across our rural district.

Type Brush / Wildland Unit
Drive 4-Wheel Drive
Tank 250 Gallons

  • Initial brush & grass fire attack

  • Support to state forestry on wildland fires
