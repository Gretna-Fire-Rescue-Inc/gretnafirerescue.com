Why It Matters

Your Gift Saves Lives

Gretna Fire & Rescue is a combination volunteer and career department that has protected this community since 1935. We rely heavily on the generous support of residents and businesses in our district to maintain the equipment and training that keeps our crews ready to respond.

Unlike larger city departments, our funding does not come from a dedicated tax base. Donations from the community we serve are essential to bridging the gap between what government funding provides and what it truly takes to protect lives and property in Pittsylvania County.

Your contribution — in any amount — directly supports the men and women who answer the call when your family needs help most.

Tax Deductible Gretna Fire & Rescue is a recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. Please retain your receipt for tax purposes.

Where It Goes

What Your Gift Funds

01

Life-Saving Equipment

Modern firefighting and EMS gear is expensive and must be regularly replaced to meet safety standards. Your donation keeps our crews equipped and protected.

  • Turnout gear and self-contained breathing apparatus
  • AEDs, stretchers, and advanced airway equipment
  • Hoses, nozzles, and suppression tools
  • Vehicle maintenance and fleet readiness
02

Training & Certification

Well-trained responders save lives. Donations fund the ongoing education that keeps our volunteers and career staff current with the latest protocols and certifications.

  • Firefighter I & II certification courses
  • EMT and advanced EMS training programs
  • Continuing education and skills refreshers
  • State and national certification fees
03

Community Programs

Prevention and education are just as important as response. We invest in programs that make our community safer every day.

  • Fire prevention and public education outreach
  • Smoke detector installation for residents in need
  • School and youth safety programs
  • Community events and open houses
