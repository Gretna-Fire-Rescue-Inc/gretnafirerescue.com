Department Updates

News & Announcements

June 2026

New Medic Unit Placed in Service

Gretna Fire & Rescue is proud to announce the delivery and commissioning of a new Type I ALS ambulance. The unit expands our front-line EMS capability and will replace an aging reserve unit. The apparatus was funded through a combination of community donations and grant awards.

Announcement
May 2026

Six Members Complete Paramedic Certification

Congratulations to six GFR members who successfully completed their National Registry Paramedic certification this spring. Their dedication to advanced training strengthens our ALS capability across both medic units and ensures the highest level of pre-hospital care for our community.

News
April 2026

Annual Inspection Results — All Apparatus Compliant

All GFR apparatus passed the annual state inspection with no deficiencies noted. Our maintenance team and apparatus committee work year-round to ensure every unit is operationally ready and meets NFPA and state compliance standards.

Update
March 2026

GFR Receives Pittsylvania County Community Award

Gretna Fire & Rescue was recognized by Pittsylvania County for outstanding community service and emergency response during 2025. The department was honored at the County Board of Supervisors meeting for its commitment to protecting lives and property throughout District 22.

News
January 2026

2025 Annual Report: Over 800 Calls Responded

Gretna Fire & Rescue responded to over 800 emergency calls in 2025, including fire, EMS, motor vehicle accidents, and technical rescue incidents. We thank our volunteers and career staff for their dedication and our community for their continued support.
