Protecting Gretna For Over 91 Years

Gretna Fire & Rescue is a combination volunteer and career department serving the citizens of Gretna, Virginia and the surrounding Pittsylvania County communities. Built on a foundation of faith, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to training — our members stand ready around the clock.

Privacy Policy

Overview

Gretna Fire & Rescue ("GFR," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our newsletter and text alert signup, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

  • Your first and last name (optional)
  • Your email address (required for the newsletter)
  • Your mobile phone number (only if you opt in to text alerts)

How We Use It

  • Send our email newsletter, if you subscribed
  • Send SMS/text alerts, only if you separately checked the text alert box
  • Confirm your subscription via a double opt-in email

We never sell or rent your name, email, or phone number to third parties.

Text Message (SMS) Program

Consent to receive texts is separate from consent to receive our email newsletter — checking one box does not opt you into the other. If you opt in to texts: message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, reply STOP at any time to opt out, and consent is never a condition of any purchase or service.

Cookies & Tracking

Our public website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Your Choices
