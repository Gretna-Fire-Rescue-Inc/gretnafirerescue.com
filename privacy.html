Privacy Policy

How Gretna Fire & Rescue collects and uses information from our website, newsletter, and text alert program.

Effective Date: September 28, 2026

Overview

Gretna Fire & Rescue ("GFR," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through gretnafirerescue.com, how we use it, and the choices you have — particularly regarding our email newsletter and SMS/text alert program.

This policy covers the public website (gretnafirerescue.com) only. Our Member Portal (portal.gretnafirerescue.com) is a separate, login-based system used for department operations and has its own data-handling practices as part of your membership.

Information We Collect

When you sign up for our newsletter or text alerts, we collect:

  • Your first and last name (optional)
  • Your email address (required for the newsletter)
  • Your mobile phone number (only if you choose to sign up for text alerts)

We do not require any of this information to browse the website generally — it is only collected if you voluntarily submit our newsletter/text alert signup form.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send you our email newsletter, if you subscribed
  • Send you SMS/text alerts, only if you separately checked the box to opt in to text messages
  • Confirm your subscription (we use a double opt-in process — you must click a confirmation link in an email before you're added to our mailing list)
  • Respond to questions you send us

We do not sell, rent, or share your name, email, or phone number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Text Message (SMS) Program

Consent to receive text messages is separate from consent to receive our email newsletter. Checking one box does not opt you into the other. You can be on the email list without being on the text list, and vice versa.

If you opt in to our text alert program:

  • You will receive recurring automated text messages from Gretna Fire & Rescue at the phone number you provide
  • Message frequency varies depending on department news, events, and alerts
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message, or reply HELP for help
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service
  • Not all mobile carriers are supported; carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages

Your phone number is used solely to deliver the text alert program and is not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies & Tracking

Our public website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. Some pages may use your browser's local storage for basic, per-device convenience (such as remembering a dismissed banner) — this information stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy.

Your Choices

  • Email newsletter: every email we send includes an unsubscribe link
  • Text alerts: reply STOP to any text message at any time to opt out
  • Delete your information: email us at info@gretnafirerescue.com and we will remove your contact information from our newsletter/text alert list

Children's Privacy

Our website and newsletter/text alert signup are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Links

Our website links to other services we use, including our Member Portal, PayPal (for donations), and social media platforms. Those services have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review — we are not responsible for their practices.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Effective Date" above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our newsletter or text alert program after a change means you accept the updated policy.
