About the Space

A Great Venue for Your Community

Gretna Fire & Rescue makes its station hall available to the community for private events, meetings, and celebrations. The rental program is one of the ways we give back — and your rental fees help fund our ongoing operations and equipment needs.

The main hall offers ample open floor space suitable for banquets, receptions, birthday parties, reunions, and community meetings. The hall is equipped with tables and chairs, a kitchen for food service, and on-site parking for your guests.

We ask that all renters respect the working nature of our station. Our crews remain on duty during your event and apparatus bays are not part of the rental space. Please review the rental guidelines below before submitting your request.

What's Included

Hall Amenities

🪑

Tables & Chairs

Tables and folding chairs for up to 150 guests are available on-site. Setup and breakdown is the renter's responsibility. Additional arrangement configurations are welcome.

🍽️

Kitchen Access

A shared kitchen is included with your rental and equipped with a range, refrigerator, and prep space. Renters are responsible for supplying all food, beverages, and serving ware.

🅿️

On-Site Parking

Free off-street parking is available adjacent to the station for your guests. Parking availability may vary depending on active department operations.

Rental Guidelines

What's Allowed & What's Not

Permitted

  • ✓ Private parties, birthday celebrations, and family reunions
  • ✓ Community meetings, civic events, and nonprofit gatherings
  • ✓ Catered meals and food service in designated kitchen area
  • ✓ Decorations that do not require nails, staples, or damage to walls
  • ✓ Music at a reasonable volume appropriate to the event
  • ✓ Alcohol with prior written approval from department leadership

Not Permitted

  • ✗ Access to apparatus bays, equipment rooms, or crew quarters
  • ✗ Open flames, candles, or any fire-based decorations inside the hall
  • ✗ Smoking or vaping anywhere on station property
  • ✗ Exceeding the posted occupancy limit of 150 persons
  • ✗ Subleasing or transferring the rental agreement to another party
  • ✗ Events that interfere with active emergency response operations
