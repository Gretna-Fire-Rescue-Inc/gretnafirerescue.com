What Is a Dedicated Standby?

EMS On-Site for Your Event

A dedicated EMS standby means a Gretna Fire & Rescue ambulance and crew are physically stationed at your event for its duration — ready to respond immediately if a medical emergency occurs. Rather than waiting for a 911 call to be dispatched, help is already on scene.

Standbys are ideal for sporting events, festivals, concerts, fairs, races, and any large gathering where the risk of injury or medical emergency is elevated. Having dedicated EMS coverage demonstrates a commitment to participant and spectator safety.

Because GFR's primary obligation is always to 911 calls, dedicated coverage is subject to crew and vehicle availability. If a crew is diverted during your event, a replacement will be sent as soon as one is available.

The Process

How to Request Coverage

01

Download & Complete

Submit the Dedicated Standby Service Agreement online through our member portal. Provide your event name, dates, times, location, organization, and contact details — all fields are required.

02

Sign & Submit

Sign the agreement and return it to GFR by email or mail no later than 14 days before your event. GFR will review the request and confirm availability. No coverage is guaranteed until GFR has confirmed.

03

Coverage & Billing

On the event day, an ambulance and crew will be on-site for the agreed period. After the event, GFR will invoice you for all hours of service. Payment is due within 30 days of receiving the invoice.

Coverage Details

What's Included

🚑

Ambulance & Crew

Each standby unit consists of an emergency vehicle and two certified technicians, including at least one EMT-Basic. The crew will position themselves at your event and remain dedicated to it for the contracted period.

⚕️

ALS Availability

The assigned vehicle may or may not be equipped for Advanced Life Support (ALS) care on-site. However, ALS services are available elsewhere in the county and can be dispatched to the event if needed.

📻

Radio Communications

If coordination with on-site medical staff is required, event radios will be provided to GFR providers for the duration of the standby. Discuss this need when submitting your agreement.

Agreement Terms

Key Terms & Conditions

01

Dedicated Coverage

GFR will station an ambulance and two technicians (min. 1 EMT-Basic) at your event for the agreed dates, times, and location specified in the agreement.

02

Subject to Availability

Dedicated standbys are subject to the availability of EMS crews and spare emergency vehicles at the time of the event.

03

911 Calls Take Priority

If a life-threatening emergency requires the dedicated crew to respond elsewhere, another ambulance will be routed to your event upon availability.

04

Rate

The standby fee is $100 per hour, per vehicle assigned to the event.

05

Invoicing & Payment

GFR will invoice the service user upon completion of standby services. Payment is due within 30 days of receiving the invoice.

06

14-Day Submission Deadline

GFR reserves the right to refuse any agreement submitted less than 14 days before the event start time.

07

Cancellation

Either party may cancel the agreement by providing at least 48 hours advance notice before the scheduled event.

08

Standard of Care

Nothing in the agreement creates a higher standard of care than that recognized under the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia for EMS services.

09

Patient Transport Billing

Standby fees cover only GFR's on-site availability. Normal charges for patient care and transport are billed separately to the patient.

10

Event Radios

If communication with on-site medical staff is required, event radios will be provided to GFR providers for use during the standby where applicable.
